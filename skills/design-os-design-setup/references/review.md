The workspace review. Read at step 6. Run it as a designer opening the workspace cold: someone who knows Clarity and Alloy but wasn't in the setup conversation. Where a subagent is available, the `design-workspace-review` agent runs this for genuinely fresh eyes, and then follows through on the organisation.

## What to check

1. **Flow coverage.** Walk the brief's flow (its diagram, or the user journey implied by its requirements) step by step. For each step, is there a board, a copied frame, or a deliberate "no change"? List steps with no screen.
2. **States.** For each board: empty, loading, error, success, permission (who can't see or do it), and the edge states the brief names (mid-transaction, institution non-admin, low suitability, and so on). List missing states as board suggestions.
3. **Organisation.** Judge it against `references/organize.md` (What good organisation looks like).
4. **Notes.** Every copied frame has stickies; every NEW board says what it starts from; every other team's screen says "needs <team> sign-off"; every paste-in has a working link.
5. **Freshness.** Copied frames built for a superseded decision are labelled with what changed. Anything marked "shipped" really matches the live product, as far as the sources show.
6. **First ten minutes.** Could a designer start the first board in ten minutes from Start here alone? If not, what's the one thing missing?

## Report, then follow through

Show the findings in this order:

- **Gaps:** flow steps or states with no screen, each with a suggested board ID and group.
- **Organisation plan:** from `references/organize.md` step 3, before and after.
- **Fixes:** small things to correct (a missing sticky, a dead link, an unlabeled superseded frame).

Ask for one yes covering the organisation plan, the new boards for gaps, and the fixes; the person can strike any of them. Then apply what they agreed, following `references/organize.md`, and report what changed.

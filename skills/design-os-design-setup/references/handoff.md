The handoff package for the Designer route. Read at step 5. Moved here from `design-os-design-impact` in 1.6.0.

The package gives the owning team's Design lead everything the impact run and the setup found, so they can pick the work up without redoing the research. It's a starting point for their judgment, not a design decision.

## Find the Design lead

Use `pm-os-clarity-context` People lookup, fresh, on every run:

1. Resolve each affected surface to its owning team (`org/ownership`), then the team's file for its triad roles.
2. Fetch the Product Team Directory and read the current Design lead for that team.
3. If the directory can't be reached, fall back to Linear team leads, and to who led the feature's Design-team projects or authored its frames, and say that's the source. If its entry is blank, "(fill in)" or "(verify)", or the page was last reviewed more than 6 months ago, say so and raise it as an open item.
4. Cross-team work: name the Design lead of the team that owns the main home, and list the other teams' Design leads under the screens they own.

Name the person in the package you write, because it's for them. Never write a name into this skill's files, its evals, or anything saved for reuse.

## What goes in it

1. **The work:** the brief link (with Doc Stage and last-edited date), the Linear item, and one line on what it does.
2. **Primary workspace:** the link from step 4, first and prominent, plus anything it supersedes.
3. **Designs to base this on:** each prior-work find with its link, status (shipped, ready for dev, exploration, superseded) and which boards it feeds.
4. **Who picks it up:** the Design leads, with the source of each name.
5. **Why it needs a designer:** the route signals from the readout, each tied to a screen.
6. **Affected screens:** the readout table, with "needs <team> sign-off" on cross-team changes.
7. **Extend candidates and Alloy gaps:** each new pattern, Alloy gap, new section or non-web channel, with the closest Alloy match. Word gaps so they can go straight to #support-alloy-design-system.
8. **Open questions:** the brief's open items that touch design, plus anything unsettled (no reference, inferred owner).
9. **Still manual:** the paste-ins and who to ask.
10. **Placement:** map version, main home card link, Map suggestion lines.
11. **Suggested next skills:** for example `design-os-research-patterns` for an Extend pattern, `design-os-fill-mock-data` and `design-os-stress-test-design` on the boards, `design-os-prep-eng-handoff` later.

Keep it skimmable. Link, don't paste: the brief, the frames and the map card are all links.

## Where it goes

Offer a Notion page (on the initiative record if one exists, otherwise a private draft) or a Linear comment, under the Linear write rule in `pm-os-clarity-context`. Show the title or comment first and write only after a yes. Return the link the tool gives back. Don't draft a Slack message.
